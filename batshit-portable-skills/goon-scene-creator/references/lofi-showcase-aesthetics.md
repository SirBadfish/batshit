# Lofi Showcase Aesthetics

Use this reference for cozy, cinematic, lofi, and social-showcase Goon scenes.

## Goal

A good Goon showcase scene should feel like a place the avatar belongs, not a flat wallpaper. Build a simple readable stage with:

- one strong mood;
- a clear floor plane;
- believable scale around the Goon;
- foreground, midground, and background depth;
- lighting that frames the Goon without washing out the avatar.

## Composition

Prefer a practical room layout:

- floor and at least two wall directions for spatial grounding;
- one visual anchor such as a desk, bed, window, balcony, altar, console, or couch;
- one outside view or skybox cue that explains the world;
- a few props that imply a story;
- enough empty space for standing, sitting, or lying motions.

Do not fill every corner. The Goon needs breathing room.

## Lighting

Strong lofi/showcase lighting often uses contrast:

- warm interior lamp plus cool rainy window;
- neon outside plus dark quiet room;
- sunrise/sunset rim light plus soft indoor fill;
- moonlight plus candles, monitor glow, or fairy lights.

Keep the Goon readable. Avoid all-backlit scenes where the avatar becomes a silhouette unless the user asked for that.

## Texture Style

The skybox and room textures should feel detailed up close without becoming noisy. Useful words:

- lived-in;
- tactile materials;
- subtle wear;
- soft dust;
- natural imperfections;
- crisp fine texture;
- high-frequency detail;
- realistic fabric, wood grain, plaster, glass, metal, or stone.

Avoid generic smooth plastic surfaces unless the scene is intentionally futuristic.

## Scene Seeds

Use these as original starting points, not as copies of existing art:

| Mood | Scene idea |
|---|---|
| Rainy lofi | Small apartment studio, wet city skyline outside, warm desk lamp, headphones, books, soft reflections. |
| Cozy fantasy | Wooden room above an old shop, moonlit window, herbs, candles, low bed, worn rugs, magical but subtle props. |
| Cyber night | Compact room above a neon street, glass wall, console glow, dense cables, clean walking space. |
| Desert sunrise | Open adobe room, pale skybox, woven rugs, clay walls, warm low-angle sunlight. |
| Forest shrine | Small covered platform, mossy stone, lanterns, distant trees in the skybox, simple sitting mat. |
| Starship cabin | Compact cabin with window to stars, practical panels, quiet bed or chair, cool rim lights. |

## Prompt Add-Ons

For lofi scenes:

```txt
cozy lived-in lofi room, warm lamp light, cool rainy exterior, tactile materials, realistic fine surface detail, calm cinematic composition
```

For fantasy scenes:

```txt
subtle magical atmosphere, handmade wooden interior, soft candlelight, worn rugs, moonlit window, natural imperfections, crisp material detail
```

For sci-fi scenes:

```txt
compact futuristic room, practical panels, soft screen glow, reflective surfaces, clean readable floor space, high-detail metal and glass textures
```

## Creator-Respect Boundaries

Use references to understand:

- color palette;
- lighting;
- mood;
- room density;
- camera feel.

Do not copy:

- exact room layouts;
- branded props;
- character designs;
- channel overlays;
- named franchise worlds;
- distinctive artist compositions.

When a user provides a recognizable reference, describe the new scene as "inspired by the mood" and make the actual layout and details original.
